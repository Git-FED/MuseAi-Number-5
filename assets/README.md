# Art assets

`icon-source.svg` is the editable five-stripe mark. `icon.png` and `og-image.png` are raster art used for the Android launcher/app identity and link previews. The eight standalone comic illustrations are in `../promo/` and are included individually in the landing page.

The mascot and promo drawings communicate the product direction only. They do not establish that five active agent sessions, durable cookies, encrypted local storage, or isolation have passed testing. See `docs/TESTING.md` and `docs/DECISIONS.md`.
