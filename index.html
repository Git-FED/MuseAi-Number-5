Switching, minus a ceremony

Pick a tab.
Let the little door open.

The intended flow keeps five named places in one Android shell. The app sources are a starter implementation; lifecycle, persistence, and isolation still need their documented device tests.

The aspiration board

All five awake.
None asking for a fresh cup of context.

This is the concept, not a measured performance guarantee. The app has to prove how it retains or restores each workspace before that line graduates from joke to promise.

A promise that needs proof

Separate worlds.
Not just different hats.

Per-slot storage separation is a core requirement, not something to infer from the art. Cookie, local-storage, cache, process, backup, and reset behavior must be validated on supported Android versions.

Tab six · Group

A shared room with the send button still in your hands.

The planned Group screen is expected to show relay messages, an explicit not-connected state, and poll every 60 seconds only when a real relay is configured. The included Android screen is a placeholder and sends nothing. The sample Group flow is read-only and never sends.
